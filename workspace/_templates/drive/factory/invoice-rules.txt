Invoice rules

Every coach's invoices use one layout, fixed in scripts/issue.py. The only thing that changes from coach to coach is the coach's phone and email
in the header. Coaches can read this file; only the head coach changes it. A coach's Claude never makes an invoice any
other way.

What's on every invoice, top to bottom

| Part | Content | From |
|---|---|---|
| Title | INVOICE (left) · trading name (right) | factory/business |
| Header | trading name · website · the coach's phone · the coach's email | business, the coach's me |
| Bill-to box | BILL TO: the client's full name and phone · INVOICE # · ISSUED · DUE | clients; the generator |
| Items | ITEM · PRICE · QUANTITY · AMOUNT. One row per item per month, e.g. *<item> - November × 4* | the coach's draft × factory/billable-work |
| Totals | Subtotal · Total · Amount Due | the generator |
| Payment Instruction | the business's payment wording, asking the client to use the invoice number as their reference, then account holder, bank, branch, account | business |
| Sign-off | POP request · thank you · team sign-off | business |

Font: Inter (from invoice-template/fonts/), black and grey on white. No VAT line while not VAT-registered.

Numbers and the payment reference

- Invoice number = payment reference: year-coach number-invoice number, e.g. 2026-03-490
  (invoice_number_format in business: {year}-{coach}-{seq}).
- Coach number: each coach has their own two-digit number (coach_number in their me), never shared and never
  reused. That's what makes every reference unique across the business. The bank recon flags any two coaches sharing one.
- Invoice number: each coach's own running sequence, carried on from their old app (invoice_start in me).
  The generator gives out the next number. Coaches never choose one.
- The bank recon recognises the reference with or without the year and dashes: 2026-03-490, 2026 03 490, 03-490.
- Behind the scenes each invoice also gets an id with the coach's code (<coach code>-2026-0490). If a reference ever fits more
  than one client, the recon asks the head coach instead of guessing.
- The invoice asks clients to use it: payment_instruction in business contains {reference}, which the generator
  replaces with the invoice number.

Rules

1. One invoice per client per run. Each item's billed setting in billable-work decides when it's billed: in advance
   (up to next month) or after (the month past).
2. Dated the run's date (invoice_day in business), due the same day.
3. Items and prices come only from factory/billable-work. An item that isn't there can't be billed.
4. Never edit an issued invoice by hand. While it's unpaid, the coach updates it through the generator: same number and date, new PDF and
   list. Once it's paid it stays as it is, and the coach adjusts the client's future invoices. There are no credit notes.
5. PDFs first, then the list, in one step. A run isn't done until its list exists.
6. One run, one list. A second list in the same month (2026-10-2) is only for an updated invoice, or for something genuinely missed (--extra).
