Business

Everything on an invoice that's the same for every coach. Only the head coach edits this.
What changes per coach (their phone and email in the invoice header) lives in each coach's me.

- payment_instruction: {reference} is replaced with the invoice's number, so clients are asked to pay with it.
- invoice_number_format: how the invoice number is printed; it's also the payment reference. {year} is the invoice year,
  {coach} the coach's own number (coach_number in their me), {seq} the coach's running invoice number:
  2026-03-490. Unique across all coaches.
- invoice_day: the day of the month invoices go out. Each month's invoicing run is due that day.
- payment_terms: invoices are due on receipt, so the due date is the invoice date and days unpaid count from it.
